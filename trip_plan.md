# North Spain RV Trip Plan

**Route:** Osnabrück → France transit → Basque Country → Santander work base → Cantabria → Picos de Europa → Asturias → France transit → Zürich  
**Travel window:** Friday 22 May, after 14:00 → Saturday 6 June target arrival in Zürich  
**Buffer:** Sunday 7 June + Monday 8 June before work resumes  
**Vehicle:** Hummer RV  
**Travellers:** 2 adults, possibly with bikes  

This version is built around real visit time, realistic RV driving, and the fixed work requirement on **Thursday 28 May morning**. The key logistics decision is to sleep in **Santander on Wednesday 27 May**.

Driving times are RV-adjusted estimates and exclude long breaks, traffic, parking, tolls, and campsite check-in.

## Day 5 — Tuesday 26 May — San Sebastián → Zarautz → Getaria → Zumaia → Lekeitio or Gaztelugatxe → Bilbao

**Driving:** 3–4h total  
**Sleep:** Bilbao area  
**Goal:** Basque coast day, ending at a practical Bilbao RV base.

This day is full but realistic if you start early. Do **not** try to do every optional stop.

**Drive from San Sebastián:** 30–40m

**Drive:** 10–15m  
**Visit time:** 1.5–2h

- Harbour

### Stop 3 — Zumaia Flysch
**Drive:** 15–20m  
**Visit time:** 1–1.5h

See:

### Stop 4 option A — Lekeitio
**Drive from Zumaia:** 1h–1h15  
**Visit time:** 1–1.5h

## Day 11 — Monday 1 June — Covadonga Lakes + Cangas de Onís

**Route link:** [Open Day 11 in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=43.35,-5.1294&destination=43.35,-5.1294&travelmode=driving&waypoints=43.3093,-5.0557%7C43.2695,-4.9881)  
**Driving:** minimal if using shuttle  
**Sleep:** Cangas / Arenas de Cabrales  
**Goal:** Picos de Europa / Covadonga day.

Important: **1 June is inside the 2026 restricted-access period for Covadonga Lakes**, so plan to use the shuttle/bus system rather than driving the RV up.

### Plan
- Early start from Cangas.
- Park at designated parking / bus area.
- Shuttle to Covadonga Lakes.
- Walk the lake loop.
- Visit Covadonga sanctuary on the way down.
- Return to Cangas.

### Visit duration
- Lakes + loop: 3–4h
- Sanctuary: 45m–1h
- Total with buses: 5–6h

### Bike note
Bicycles are not affected by the Covadonga access restrictions, but this is a serious climb, not a casual ride.
