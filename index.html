About the Trip

Welcome to Espain — your central hub for planning and remembering our adventure through Spain. Use this page to keep track of destinations, itineraries, tips, and anything else that makes the trip unforgettable.

Destinations

  • 🏙️ Madrid – Museums, tapas, and the Retiro park
  • 🌊 Barcelona – Gaudí, La Boqueria, and the beach
  • 🎶 Seville – Flamenco, the Alcázar, and orange trees
  • 🏔️ Granada – The Alhambra and Sacromonte caves

Quick Tips

  • 🍽️ Lunch is the main meal — restaurants fill up between 2–4 pm
  • 🌞 Summers are hot; pack sunscreen and a hat
  • 🚄 The AVE high-speed rail connects major cities quickly
  • 💶 Tipping is appreciated but not mandatory
